What happens next

  1. This form emails whatsontoowoomba@agentmail.to through FormSubmit.
  2. An hourly check reads that inbox. A valid listing is added to data/submissions.json and shown on the calendar. Nobody approves it by hand.
  3. Every listing needs a suburb or address. Events and garage sales also need a start time. A garage sale needs a list of items.
  4. The check drops spam: a filled honeypot, over-long text, too many links, shorteners, profanity, duplicates, listings outside Toowoomba, and anything already finished.
