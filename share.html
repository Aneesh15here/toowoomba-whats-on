What happens next

  1. This form emails Aneesh at whatsontoowoomba@agentmail.to through FormSubmit.
  2. Aneesh checks that the listing is real and suitable.
  3. Approved items are added by hand to data/submissions.json with "status": "approved".
  4. The site merges that file with the collected listings. The collector never publishes a submission on its own.

FormSubmit shows a captcha, then returns you to the thanks page. Sending a listing does not put it on the calendar.
