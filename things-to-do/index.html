Toowoomba, Queensland

Things to do in Toowoomba

Things to do in Toowoomba, Queensland: walks, parks, lookouts, scenic drives, and Saturday parkrun. Toowoomba parkrun starts at 7:00 am at Queens Park on Lindsay Street, East Toowoomba. Highfields parkrun starts at the same time at Highfields Sports and Recreation Park, 220 Barracks Road. Dated concerts, markets, and classes are on the calendar.

Outdoor events

Queens Park and Botanic Gardens

East Toowoomba

Heritage gardens and lawns beside the CBD, playgrounds and barbecues, and the ground for the Carnival of Flowers.

Spring Bluff railway station

Spring Bluff

A heritage station and gardens about 15 minutes north of the city, along Murphy's Creek Road.

Drive to Pittsworth

Pittsworth

About 40 km southwest across farming country, with a historical village, gallery and a small main street.

Hikes and walks

Table Top Mountain walk

Rangeville

A steep Grade 5 return walk to the flat summit. The route is not maintained, so wear boots and take care.

Lookouts

Picnic Point lookout

Rangeville

The escarpment lookout over the Lockyer Valley, with playgrounds, picnic lawns and short walks in the parklands.

Lookouts

Crows Nest Falls

Crows Nest National Park

Cascades and a 20 metre waterfall in eucalypt forest, about 50 km north of Toowoomba. Stay behind the lookout barriers.

Family and kids

Toowoomba City

Formal gardens, a scented garden, a playground and picnic lawns a short walk from the centre.

Queensland Museum Cobb+Co

East Toowoomba

A transport and social history museum beside Queens Park. Open daily from 9:00 am to 4:00 pm. Admission is charged. It closes on Good Friday, Anzac Day, Christmas Day, and Boxing Day.
